1. Authority to Request and Obtain Information:

I/We, the undersigned client, hereby nominate, constitute, and appoint Royal Square Financial (Pty) Ltd (FSP License No. 29370) and its authorized representative, Qiniso Ntuli, with full legal power and authority to act on my behalf to request, obtain, inspect, receive, and review all relevant information, documentation, policy schedules, investment summaries, retirement annuity records, employee benefit statements, medical scheme details, and tax certificates pertaining to my financial portfolio.

2. Direction to Financial Institutions:

I hereby instruct and direct all registered long-term insurers, short-term insurers, collective investment scheme management companies, banks, retirement fund administrators, and administrative financial services providers to furnish Royal Square Financial with all requested information, fund values, surrender values, historical premium schedules, and contractual conditions as they may reasonably require in execution of this mandate.

3. Purpose of this Mandate:

This consent is granted specifically for the purpose of enabling Royal Square Financial to perform a comprehensive financial needs analysis, risk profiling, and holistic financial planning review in terms of Section 8 of the General Code of Conduct under the Financial Advisory and Intermediary Services Act (FAIS Act, No. 37 of 2002). This represents Step 1 of the Six-Step Financial Planning Process.

4. Explicit Limitation of Authority:

This consent constitutes an informational authority only. It does not authorize Royal Square Financial or its representatives to alter, amend, switch, encumber, surrender, or cancel any existing policy, investment, or contract without my separate, explicit, subsequent written instruction.

5. Protection of Personal Information (POPIA):

Royal Square Financial undertakes to treat all personal and financial data obtained under this mandate with the highest standard of confidentiality, in strict compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA, Act No. 4 of 2013) and FAIS regulatory requirements.

6. Validity of Electronic Copy:

An electronic copy, digital transmission, or verified facsimile of this executed consent shall be deemed valid, binding, and enforceable as the original document by all financial institutions.
